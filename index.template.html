Forecast source & assumptions

Hourly weather is requested along your projected GPX positions, using the route elevation for model downscaling. The outlook starts from your last route kilometre and current time, or your planned race time before the start. Sync location & time to update your progress.

Rain totals are approximate hourly samples along a moving route. Rain chance is the largest hourly probability, not a probability for the entire window. Mountain wind, cloud and exposure can differ from the model; distant forecasts have less detail. Missing values stay unavailable. Saved forecasts retain their original times and locations.

Wind arrows show where the air is blowing, with north at the top. “From” labels give the wind’s source: wind from N points south. The summary direction belongs to the strongest hourly 10 m wind sample. Wind and gust speeds are shown in km/h. Gust peaks may occur at a different stop.

Sources & assumptions

Opening hours researched 25 October 2026 from owner pages and OpenStreetMap. Restaurant hours do not establish kitchen service. Water points have unverified operability. Fuel stations are not food shops unless a shop is separately mapped.

ETAs are planning estimates. Normal stops are included in daily targets. Add extra walking, waiting or rest under Extra delay if beyond your normal allowance. Arrival windows use ±5 km/day scenarios, not probabilities. Access is treated as resolved.

Settings & help

Hold the phone upright and turn to look around. Drag the view for a temporary offset; Recenter clears that offset. The grid is a reference plane, not terrain.

Compass reference can be magnetic or unspecified by the browser. No true-north correction is assumed. Move away from metal objects if readings seem wrong. Depending on your phone, calibration may involve moving it in a figure-eight pattern. This app cannot calibrate the magnetometer. Use Manual heading or Follow route if needed.

A hint helps distinguish repeated route passes. Backtracking is supported. GPS position remains authoritative when the route match is clear.

SIMULATION — not your live position

GPS and compass need HTTPS and browser permission. There is no camera or video. Offline HTML contains the race GPX and all preview code. An uploaded GPX is kept only for this session.
