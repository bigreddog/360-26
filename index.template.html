Sources, assumptions & how this roadbook works

Opening-hour checks: owner pages and OpenStreetMap data researched 25 October 2026. Weekday and split sessions are evaluated for the actual arrival date. Unsupported seasonal/holiday rules remain unknown until you add a simpler confirmed schedule. Restaurant hours do not establish kitchen service. Mapped drinking-water points have unverified operability. Fuel stations are not counted as food shops unless separately mapped as such.

All ETAs are planning estimates. Net daily targets include normal stops; detours, waiting and additional delays consume the finish margin. The last daily target repeats if the race takes longer than four days. Access is treated as resolved, as requested. This app uses the candidate GPX supplied in this project.
